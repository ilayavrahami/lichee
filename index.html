05 / THE ARTIST

WHO IS
LICHEE?

Lichee is a pop artist creating cinematic, emotional music that explores the space between what people see and what we hide beneath the surface.

Her sound blends soft pop, synth-driven production and intimate storytelling, turning personal emotions into songs that feel both vulnerable and powerful.

Through her music, Lichee explores themes of love, identity, loneliness and the stories we keep behind closed doors — creating songs that feel personal, yet deeply relatable.
